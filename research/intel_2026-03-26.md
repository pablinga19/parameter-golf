## Promising Compression Techniques

### rANS entropy coding (Priority 1)
- INT6 (6 BPW) + rANS → ~3.5 BPW = 42% artifact savings
- Libs: craystack (numpy), DFloat11 (production)
- Student-t prior (nu=3-6) best for transformer weights
- 2-3 days, low risk

### Leech Lattice VQ (arXiv:2603.11021, Mar 11 2026)
- 24D sphere packing at 2 BPW, 92% Shannon limit
- Adoul-Barth nearest-neighbor via extended Golay code
- No official code yet. C impl: github.com/avanpo/leech-decoding
- 512d not divisible by 24 (needs padding)
- 5-7 days, high risk but massive upside

### DCT weight codec
- JPEG-style 2D-DCT on weight matrices
- 80% energy in top 20% coefficients for smooth matrices
- scipy.fft.dctn exists
- Nobody has tried this in the competition
- 3-4 days, low-medium risk

### SeedLM (arXiv:2410.10714)
- LFSR seed + coefficients reconstruct weight blocks
- No code, FPGA-focused, not viable as-is
- Salvageable only as storage format with startup reconstruction
